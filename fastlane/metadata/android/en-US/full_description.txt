History's icons are highschoolers with a pogo stick, and they're stuck in one cursed open world: the Forever Realm.

<b>Dig, build and fight</b>
Mine a procedurally generated world, craft swords, pickaxes and alchemy brews, build a homestead with a bed, chests and campfires, and battle through four elemental realms to win the relics that open the Forever Gate.

<b>Everything happens in the world</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Your hero:</b> pick one of eight highschoolers. Their perk becomes a combat stat, and each hero levels to 20 with mastery tiers, style combos, guard pips and revives.</li>
<li><b>Pogs as gear:</b> equip collectible pogs for stats, and fire their abilities from the hotbar: freeze, slammer throw, ground pound, shield, speed, plunder and double jump.</li>
<li><b>Pog duels:</b> slam against Schoolyard rivals and eleven Circuit pros hidden across the map. Play Friendly, or For keeps with a stake.</li>
<li><b>Yoyo tricks:</b> craft yoyos and flick trick sequences mid-fight. Twenty tricks, and fumbles snap strings.</li>
<li><b>Pogo stick and Dash Trials:</b> bounce, super-bounce and stomp through gate courses for medals and a Pogo Rank.</li>
<li><b>Circuit Arena:</b> climb a ladder of sixty-second bouts, each with its pro's own rules.</li>
<li><b>Quest rifts:</b> fifteen platformer levels with bosses, a rival racer and co-op, torn into the overworld.</li>
</ul>

<b>Risk and reward</b>
XP only counts once you rest or win a realm; die and you lose half of what you carry. Stakes, Hardcore runs and Showboat bouts trade safety for bigger rewards.

Touch controls, keyboard and Xbox controllers are supported. No ads, no tracking, no accounts: your saves stay on your device.
